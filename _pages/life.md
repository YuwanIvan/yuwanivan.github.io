Obtuse Coffee (钝角咖啡)
=========================

During my Ph.D. at Peking University, I worked as a barista and served as the founding accountant of **Obtuse Coffee (钝角咖啡)**, a student-founded and student-operated café at the School of Mathematical Sciences.

{% include gallery id="coffee_gallery" caption="Obtuse Coffee at the School of Mathematical Sciences, Peking University." %}

The café was created to provide affordable, convenient, and genuinely good coffee to students and faculty working in the School’s new building. Just as importantly, it offered a comfortable place to pause, relax, and talk between seminars, problem sets, and long stretches of thinking.

For many students, Obtuse Coffee became a small anchor in everyday life. It witnessed both the frustration of being stuck on a difficult problem and more than a few “Aha!” moments. A familiar invitation around the building was:

> “走，去钝角整杯咖啡！”  
> *“Let’s go grab a coffee at Obtuse!”*

As a barista, I enjoyed making coffee and meeting people from across the School. As the founding accountant, I helped keep track of the café’s finances. Looking back, it was probably my first hands-on attempt to combine financial knowledge with inventory management,except that the inventory consisted mostly of coffee beans and milk.
